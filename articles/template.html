    Worked problem: how many hosts fit in a /26?

    A /26 leaves 32 − 26 = 6 host bits, so 26 = 64 addresses. Two are reserved (network and broadcast), leaving 62 usable hosts.

    Quick check

    How many usable host addresses are in a /30 network?

    A /30 has 2 host bits, so 4 addresses. The network and broadcast addresses are reserved, leaving 2.
